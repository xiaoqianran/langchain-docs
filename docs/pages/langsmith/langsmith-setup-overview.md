# LangSmith setup

How a LangSmith workspace is organized, where LangSmith runs, and who can do what across your organization.

LangSmith is the platform for testing, deploying, monitoring, and improving agents. This tab covers what you set up before and around that work: how a workspace organizes what you build, where LangSmith runs, and who can do what.

Start with [hosting options](/langsmith/platform-setup) if you are deciding where LangSmith runs, or with [agents](/langsmith/agents) if you are new to how LangSmith organizes a workspace.
